ReadFirst: Book Home Screen

Your home screen opens on the page you stopped at.

A calm Android home screen that puts reading first

ReadFirst replaces your home screen with the book you're reading: the last few lines, your progress, and one tap to continue. Your apps are still there, one swipe up. They just aren't the first thing you see.

Reading, built in

  • EPUB, PDF and plain-text books, with no other reader app needed
  • Text size, serif or sans, margins, and a page that remembers exactly where you were
  • PDFs trim their blank margins so text is bigger on a small screen, and follow your page colour at night

A home screen that feels like paper

  • Color ink, or Black ink: pure black on white with no animation, made for e-ink devices
  • Page colours (White, Paper, Sepia, Mist, Night) and subtle textures
  • Your library as a shelf of spines, or a stack with full titles

Free books in one tap

  • Start with a classic: Meditations, Sherlock Holmes, Pride and Prejudice, Gitanjali
  • Browse and search Project Gutenberg's 75,000+ free books
  • Connect your own Calibre, Kavita or other OPDS library

Light and private

  • Under 200 KB. No ads, no account, no tracking
  • Your books stay on your device — see the privacy policy
