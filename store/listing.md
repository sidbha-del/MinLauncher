# Play Store listing: ReadFirst

**App name (30):** ReadFirst: Book Home Screen

**Short description (80):** Unlock your phone into your book. A calm home screen that puts reading first.

**Full description:**

Your phone opens on the page you stopped at.

ReadFirst replaces your home screen with the book you're reading: the last few lines, your progress, and one tap to continue. Your apps are still there, one swipe up. They just aren't the first thing you see.

**Reading, built in**
• EPUB, PDF and plain-text books, with no other reader app needed
• Text size, serif or sans, margins, and a page that remembers exactly where you were
• PDFs trim their blank margins so text is bigger on a phone, and follow your page colour at night
• Contents, a jump bar, volume-key page turns

**A home screen that feels like paper**
• Color ink, or Black ink: pure black on white with no animation, made for e-ink phones
• Page colours (White, Paper, Sepia, Mist, Night) and subtle textures
• Your library as a shelf of spines, or a stack with full titles

**Free books in one tap**
• Start with a classic: Meditations, Sherlock Holmes, Pride and Prejudice, Gitanjali
• Browse and search Project Gutenberg's 75,000+ free books
• Connect your own Calibre, Kavita or other OPDS library

**Listening too**
• See what Audible, Libby, Play Books or Kuku FM is playing on your home screen, with play and skip (optional)
• Kindle, Play Books, Kobo and other reading apps listed in your library, one tap away

**Light and private**
• Under 200 KB. No ads, no account, no tracking
• Your books stay on your phone
• Optional: covers and author names for your own files looked up on Open Library (only the title is sent; switch off in Settings)

Switch back to your old home screen any time from Settings.
