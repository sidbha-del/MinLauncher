# Promotion: ReadFirst and MIRA, starting today

2026-09-19. Two products with different buyers: **ReadFirst** is chosen by the person who uses it; **MIRA** is chosen by a *caregiver* for someone else. Promote them separately, and link them with one founder story.

**Umbrella line:** *"A home screen should serve the person holding the phone, not the apps fighting for it."*

## Rules for every post

- Use only numbers we measured: ReadFirst's release APK is **147 KB** (release build run on a Galaxy M42), with no runtime libraries, tested on three phones. Quote a MIRA size only after building its release APK. Nothing from the Gemini plans that wasn't measured.
- The demo **is** the pitch: a 7-second screen recording, *unlock → you're on page 43*. Record it today on the Samsung in Night and Sepia.
- Nothing about MIRA's origin person (health details, names, cities) without their explicit consent.

## ReadFirst

### Today (free, under 2 hours each)

1. **The 7-second video.** Unlock → book page → turn one page → swipe up to apps. No voice-over, one caption: *"My phone opens on my book now."* Post it to Reels, Shorts, X, LinkedIn and BookTok on the same day. This single asset carries most of the plan.
2. **Recruit the 12 closed testers in public.** Turn the Play requirement into the launch: *"I need 12 people who want to read more for 14 days. You get the app first; I get your brutal feedback."* Scarcity plus a mission. Post on LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups and r/androidapps. (This also covers checklist step 5.2.)
3. **#ReadFirst 30-day build-in-public.** Post your own screen-time screenshot every few days: minutes on Instagram vs minutes in the book. Honest numbers, including bad days.

### This week

4. **E-ink phone owners: the sharpest niche.** Boox Palma, Bigme HiBreak, Hisense and Minimal Phone owners actively hunt for launchers, and Black ink (no animation, pure black and white, monochrome icons) was built for their screens. Post in r/eink, r/BooxPalma and r/dumbphones, and send the APK to 5 YouTubers who review e-ink phones. Small audience, near-certain fit, loud word of mouth.
5. **Show HN / r/androiddev: the craft story.** *"Show HN: a 147 KB Android launcher with its own EPUB engine and no libraries."* Engineers share tiny, dependency-free apps. Lead with how it's built, not with digital detox.
6. **The physical bookmark.** Print 500 bookmarks (low cost): one side *"Your phone could open on this book"*, the other a QR code to the tester sign-up. Leave them with indie bookstores, library help desks and cafés with book corners. It's where readers already are, and nobody else in the app world is doing it.

### Out-of-the-box plays

7. **"Page 1 swap" with book clubs.** A club's members set the same book as their home screen for a week and post a photo of all their phones together. One photo, 10 phones, the same page. Offer it to 3 book clubs and 2 college literary societies.
8. **Libraries' digital-detox events.** Public and college libraries run reading drives; offer a 10-minute *"make your phone a book"* setup desk. Local press loves it.
9. **Festival timing.** Diwali and New Year reading resolutions: *"The only resolution that installs itself."* Pre-schedule the posts.
10. **Unlock receipts (v1.1 feature = growth loop).** A weekly shareable card: *"Opened my book 38 times this week instead of an app grid."* Each share is an ad with the app name on it. Build it after launch, since it depends on counting opens.

### What not to do
- Don't buy installs or ads before four-week retention is known.
- Don't post in r/books with a link: self-promotion there gets banned. Share the video without a link and answer questions.

## MIRA (senior launcher)

The buyer is an adult child, often living in another city or abroad. The user is a parent who will never search the Play Store. Promote to the **caregiver**, and distribute where **phones are bought and set up**.

### Today
1. **Pilot of 10 families, run over WhatsApp.** The build installs from a shared APK (see MIRA's INSTALL notes). Ask your own network: *"Is your parent's phone confusing them? I'll set up MIRA with you on a call."* Each family becomes a case study, with their consent.
2. **A Hindi and English "set up Papa's phone in 10 minutes" video,** recorded from the caregiver's side (pairing, adding people, SOS). YouTube search is how caregivers find help.

### This month
3. **Mobile shops as the channel (out of the box).** A neighbourhood phone store sells a phone to an elderly buyer or their family: the shop sets up MIRA as a free value-add and gets a "MIRA-ready" window sticker. Start with 5 stores in two cities. This is B2B2C with no ad spend.
4. **Where seniors already are:** hearing-aid centres, eye clinics, and senior-citizen clubs and RWAs (resident welfare associations). A one-page flyer: *"Big text, calls you can see, SOS in one tap, set up by your family from anywhere."*
5. **Diaspora communities:** NRI Facebook groups and alumni groups: *"Manage your parent's phone from another country."* This is the most emotionally resonant audience.
6. **Gifting moments:** Father's Day, Mother's Day, Diwali. *"Gift a phone that's ready for them."*
7. **Credibility partners:** approach HelpAge India and Dignity Foundation for a pilot or endorsement. Don't claim clinical benefits.

### Play Store caution
MIRA's SMS, call and location permissions need Google's declaration review. Let the WhatsApp pilot and the store stories run while that review happens; don't make the launch depend on the store.
